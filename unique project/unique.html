01 — INTRODUCTION

The Price Behind Convenience

Convenience saves our time, but are we ignoring its long-term consequences?

Small choices create a bigger impact.

What Does Convenience Really Mean?

Convenience means saving time, effort and energy by making everyday tasks easier. From online shopping to instant food delivery, modern technology has transformed our lifestyle.

However, excessive convenience can also lead to packaging waste, unnecessary spending, pollution and dependence on instant services.

This project explores the hidden effects of convenience on individuals, society and the environment.

Our Key Question

Is convenience always worth its hidden cost?

What Is the Real Cost?

Every convenience has possible environmental, financial or social consequences.

Convenience Possible Hidden Cost Impact Area
